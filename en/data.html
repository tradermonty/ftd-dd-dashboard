Units

  • Columns ending in _pct in ftd_events.csv and distribution_days.csv are percent values. 1.5 means 1.5%.
  • Changes in daily_status.csv, outcome columns and most research columns are fractions: 0.015 means 1.5%. In the research files correction_pct and min_gain are percent values (3 means 3%), while min_decline_frac is a fraction (-0.002 means -0.2%). It was called min_decline_pct before and was renamed because it is a fraction.
  • pct_change means different things in two files. In distribution_days.csv it is a percent value: -0.5 is -0.5%. In daily_status.csv it is a fraction: -0.005 is -0.5%.
  • Dates are ISO 8601 session dates. Booleans are True and False, and an empty cell means not applicable.
